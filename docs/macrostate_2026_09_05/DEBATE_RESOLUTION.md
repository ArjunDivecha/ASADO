# MacroState debate and resolution

Final synthesis: 6 September 2026. The seed is preserved. This record separates arguments, evidence and design choices; independent reviewers are advisors, not additional empirical observations.

## Resolved questions

| Question | Strongest opposing argument | Final decision and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Is the concept worth researching? | ASADO already has many weak macro/regime results; another conceptual hierarchy could rename failed signals. | Yes, as three separable hypotheses: compression, conditionality and heterogeneity. Prior stored failures often concern shorter horizons or discrete regimes. They lower confidence but do not answer this matched 12M experiment. |
| Does a concept layer add information? | A fixed linear combination of primitives lies in their span. | No new information in the linear case. Include structured-ridge equivalence and primitive-plus-identical-interaction controls. Credit any gain to inductive bias or conditional structure, not information creation. |
| Must each concept pass alone? | Standalone failure could reasonably discourage more complexity. | It cannot be a universal exclusion rule: conditional effects can have weak marginal signals. Freeze a small economically motivated interaction slate; charge every variant. Prior Boundaries development results illustrate this distinction without validating the proposed strategy. |
| Should the first design be neural? | Nonlinear interactions might be the entire benefit, and strong regularization can make high dimension useful. | Keep a bounded nonlinear comparison eligible. Begin with additive and explicit-interaction controls; parameter count alone is not a valid rejection. The independent complexity-paper audit supports this qualification, not an unconditional neural recommendation. |
| Are the existing data comprehensive enough? | Most interesting states involve unreconstructable revised fundamentals. | Economic breadth is sufficient; verified replay coverage remains unresolved. Use only existing inputs and archives. Omit or conditionally label unavailable histories; do not purchase a new panel to rescue the design. |
| Are 34 markets 34 independent economies? | Country pooling exaggerates sample size and hides US/China duplication. | Use 31 macro identities for national information and grouped inference, preserving the agreed market-equal investment benchmark. Keep market-specific prices and returns separate. |
| Can global state rank markets? | A common additive scalar cancels across the cross-section. | Use global variables through predated exposures or penalized heterogeneous slopes. Explanatory global co-movement is not country-ranking evidence. |
| Can today's trade weights define history? | They may approximate slow-moving structure. | Only as explicitly conditional sensitivity. Historical reference periods with guessed publication lags are better, but not automatically original vintages. Missing lawful history makes the structural interaction not testable. |
| Should stress be divided by policy capacity? | The ratio sounds economically intuitive. | Reject a generic ratio of standardized scores: zero/negative denominators destroy meaning. Keep levels separate and use bounded conditional terms. |
| What is the fair comparator? | Concepts may beat an intentionally weak flat regression merely through shrinkage. | Primary B2 regularized same-input primitives; mandatory B3 structured ridge for the grouping claim. Engineered interactions are supplied to both representations. |
| How should annual forecasts become a portfolio? | Monthly refreshed selection may miss delayed annual payoffs; compounding overlapping labels manufactures performance. | Primary economic expression is twelve staggered, twelve-month buy-and-hold vintages with a matched vintage equal-weight benchmark. Monthly refreshed selection is secondary. Never compound annual labels as monthly P&L. |
| What counts as statistical evidence? | Ninety-six monthly annual forecasts are only a few long independent blocks. | Whole-cross-section time-block inference, paired comparisons, multiplicity adjustment, overlap diagnostics and explicit insufficient-evidence outcomes. Coverage floors do not prove power. |
| What about costs? | Turnover could make a trading strategy unattractive. | Under the user's existing research policy, all research gates are gross. Cost and turnover are implementation diagnostics only. |
| What if the hierarchy is merely interpretable? | Interpretability is itself useful. | It does not satisfy this forecasting assignment. Efficient compression may be retained only under the stated non-inferiority/stability conditions and without relabeling it incremental alpha. |

## Independent evidence reviews and changes adopted

**Prior-research review:** narrowed the interpretation of earlier failures to their actual horizon and setting; documented current harness limitations for annual labels; required grouped inference; distinguished historical reuse from untouched confirmation. Its strongest correction was the mismatch between a 12M prediction and a one-month economic kill test. The final plan adopts the matched vintage expression. It also makes B2 the fixed primary comparator, states proper non-inferiority bounds, and protects the follow-on nonlinear stage from false reuse of Stage 3 OOS.

**Literature review:** verified that the close country-index dissertation attempts causal output-gap construction, so no unsupported HP lookahead accusation remains. Its neural-versus-PCR result does not establish significant superiority at the conventional 5% level. The complexity paper prevents an automatic small-model bias, while its different target and sample prevent borrowed performance expectations.

**Data audit:** distinguishes a source-code cleaning repair from proof that stored panels were rebuilt. It identifies unfinished month-end stamps, zero-filled levels, release broadcasts, nominal vintage dates and current-versus-historical exposure issues. The candidate catalog is triage, not certification. Snapshot arithmetic confirms 10,181 annual labels internally, without elevating the data to certified replay status.

**Lead synthesis:** retains the idea while reducing the initial architectural commitment. All sixteen seed concepts receive a disposition, but four candidate blocks plus market controls define the first contest. No forecast fitting, new collection or production implementation was performed.

## Sakana review: adopted corrections and reasoned disagreements

The completed independent review used **fugu-ultra-v1.1, xhigh**. Its raw response is preserved in `SAKANA_REVIEW.md`; the lead assessment below is not a claim that Sakana verified local files. The reviewer recommended revision and no immediate confirmatory model contest.

| Sakana point | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Ninety-six overlapping annual forecast dates give too little independent history for reliable tail inference over many arms. | **Adopt the concern.** Historical development is the default. Add an inference-feasibility gate before any confirmatory wording: actual admitted history, dependence sensitivity, and null-calibration/power diagnostics for the frozen evaluation procedure. Four block-lengths of history cannot support confident bootstrap tail claims simply because the software emits a p-value. No blanket claim that 96 dates are sufficient. |
| There are mathematically at most eight independent observations, and nested tuning guarantees collapse. | **Reject the absolutes.** Eight non-overlapping annual windows are a useful warning, not an exact effective sample size for every cross-sectional IC process. Dependence can be richer or weaker. Small-sample tuning is risky, not logically impossible; use the already specified fixed-penalty exploratory route when folds are inadequate. |
| Replace forward validation with pooled generalized cross-validation. | **Reject as a default cure.** Generic pooled GCV does not automatically respect time dependence, publication clocks, or the future ranking objective. A genuinely predeclared fixed penalty is the simpler alternative. A future dependence-aware analytic selector would be a separately specified algorithm. |
| Execute at T+1 rather than assuming the preceding close is tradable. | **Clarify and adopt.** Monthly decisions remain the user's objective. Mid-month information waiting until month-end is intentional; it is not itself misalignment. Specify the first available per-market session after the common cutoff and the available price field. If existing data provide only closes, use next-session close and disclose the lag; do not invent opening prices. Report monthly index-return proxies separately. |
| Make identity-level ranking the sole primary endpoint; there are at most 25 economies. | **Reject the objective substitution and count.** There are 31 macro identities in the configured 34 tokens; 25 is a proposed coverage floor, not the full count. Preserve agreed token ranking and market-EW benchmark. Add a mandatory identity-aggregate ranking comparison and within-identity contribution decomposition before claiming cross-national transmission. A token-level gain confined to US styles is not evidence of general country transmission. |
| Performance failure after a two-month artificial lag proves PIT leakage. | **Reject that inference; retain a sensitivity.** Genuine signals decay. A common two-month delay neither proves leakage when performance falls nor rules it out when performance persists; revised levels may remain revised at every lag. Actual availability, immutable provenance and causal prefix tests are the admission controls. |
| Random concept groupings are an important placebo. | **Adopt as a predeclared descriptive mechanism check.** Freeze random seeds, group sizes, scaling and a bounded set of 100 non-selected alternative maps. Compare the economic grouping with their distribution under identical preprocessing and penalty rules. Record the diagnostic budget; never select the best random map as an uncharged new model. Similar results weaken the economic grouping story, but do not logically falsify every underlying economic mechanism. Formal p-values require a justified randomization null, not arbitrary group exchangeability. |
| Orthogonalize targets against global equity and USD factors. | **Do not change the agreed USD target.** Retain price-control ablations and factor/contribution attribution. A residual-return objective would answer a different question and needs a separately declared experiment. |
| Replay labels alone are insufficient; check real timestamp evidence. | **Agree, already required.** Record reproducible source-to-row samples stratified across countries, variables and episodes, not just names such as `first_print`. The fixed existing-data constraint applies: if evidence is absent locally, the input remains conditional or unadmitted. |

**Final synthesis after Sakana:** proceed next with the existing-input contract and evaluator design. Historical model comparisons are development evidence unless the inference gate supports stronger claims; longer or prospective evidence may be required. The model contest remains a proposed research stage, not an experiment performed in this assignment. No new user approval is required to finish this report, and no implementation authorization is inferred from the review.
